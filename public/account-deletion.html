MyBodyScan

Delete your account and data

You can permanently delete your MyBodyScan account directly in the app or request help from our support team if you cannot sign in.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to MyBodyScan.
  2. Open Settings, then Account & Privacy.
  3. Select Delete my account and follow the confirmation steps.

For security, the app may ask you to sign in again before deletion.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your account. We may need to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

The deletion process removes your MyBodyScan authentication account, profile and user records, scans, uploaded scan media, transformation previews, saved plans and preferences, and owned push-notification tokens. A directly linked Stripe customer and subscription are also cancelled and deleted where applicable.

What may be retained

Limited transaction, fraud-prevention, security, dispute, or legal records may be retained when required by law or necessary for legitimate business obligations. Apple App Store and Google Play subscriptions must be cancelled in the applicable store; deleting MyBodyScan cannot cancel a store-managed subscription.

Account deletion is permanent. Export anything you want to keep before completing the request. See our Privacy Policy for more information.
