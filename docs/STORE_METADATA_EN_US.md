**Description**

MyBodyScan connects body-progress check-ins with the daily actions that move
your routine forward.

START WITH A CLEARLY LABELED REPORT

Capture four guided views and receive a general-wellness estimate that clearly
separates information you entered, photo-based estimates, visual observations,
and calculated values. Compare valid check-ins over time under similar
conditions.

TURN INSIGHT INTO A PLAN

Build a workout plan around your goal, experience, schedule, available
equipment, and stated limitations. Track completed sessions and adjust the plan
as your routine changes. Use a workout and rest timer, log sets, reps, load, and
effort, and swap an exercise for the same movement pattern when needed.

MAKE NUTRITION PRACTICAL

Follow personalized calorie and macro targets, use a seven-day meal outline,
log meals in the units you prefer, search food databases, and scan supported
barcodes. Product insights explain the available label data and can suggest
higher-scoring choices in the same category when the data supports them. Save
private custom foods, recipes, and reusable meals. Saved allergy selections can
surface stronger warnings, but users must always verify the current label.

GET ADAPTIVE SUPPORT

Subscribers can use Personal Coach to discuss training, recovery, schedule
changes, and nutrition. A weekly review considers recovery, consistency, and
trend, shows a conservative recommendation, and changes nothing without approval.
Optional plateau check-ins and Momentum milestones focus on consistent
actions—not appearance. Eligible adults can also request a conservative
motivational Transformation Preview. It is a visualization, not a promise or
prediction.

CHOOSE THE ACCESS THAT FITS

A single scan includes the complete purchased report and its generated workout
and nutrition plan. Monthly and yearly memberships add ongoing scans, plan
history, adaptive coaching, meal logging, food insights, and progress tools.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your scan media and wellness information are tied to your account. You control
optional notifications and can export or delete your account data from
Settings.

MyBodyScan provides general wellness estimates and educational guidance. It
does not provide medical measurements, diagnoses, or treatment.
